How AR works

Three steps. No app.

Your customer uses the camera they already have. That is all it takes.

  1. 1

    Scan the QR code

    A small QR code sits on your card, menu or flyer. Customers scan it with their regular phone camera.

  2. 2

    Point at the print

    The page opens in their browser. They tap Start and point the camera at your printed design.

  3. 3

    The video plays

    Your video, offer or game plays right on top of the print. Your phone number and links are one tap away.
